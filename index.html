How to play

  1. Open the live stream on YouTube (@deepdigdaily).
  2. Type anything in the live chat — every message throws a pickaxe with your name into the mine.
  3. Dig through seven layers together and break THE CORE at the bottom. Then a new mine starts.

Every 20 likes drop TNT 💣, every 100 likes a MEGA TNT, and every new subscriber drops a nuke ☢️. Your pickaxe upgrades from wood to cosmic as you dig. Daily Grand Dig at 20:00 (Vilnius time).
